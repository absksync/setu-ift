# MEOWS (Modified Early Obstetric Warning Score) Clinical Specification

## Background & Clinical Rationale

Maternal mortality during emergency transfers is frequently preceded by subtle physiological deterioration that goes undetected or undocumented. The **Modified Early Obstetric Warning Score (MEOWS)** is a validated, rule-based clinical scoring tool adapted specifically for pregnancy and postpartum physiology (accounting for normal gestational hemodynamic changes like increased plasma volume and higher baseline pulse rate).

## Risk Stratification & Clinical Directives

### 1. High Risk (`#EF4444` / Red)
- **Criteria:** Total MEOWS Score ≥ 4 **OR** any single parameter score = 3 (Red parameter).
- **Clinical Rationale:** Imminent life-threatening decompensation (e.g. hemorrhagic shock, impending eclamptic seizure, severe sepsis, acute pulmonary edema).
- **Mandatory Action:**
  - Mobilize Obstetrician & Anesthesiologist on call immediately.
  - Crossmatch & reserve 2+ units of blood at destination blood bank.
  - Pre-stage ICU bed with continuous maternal-fetal monitoring.
  - ALS Ambulance dispatch with paramedic accompaniment.

### 2. Medium Risk (`#F59E0B` / Amber)
- **Criteria:** Total MEOWS Score 2 to 3 (and no score 3 parameter).
- **Clinical Rationale:** Significant physiological deviation (e.g. moderate pre-eclampsia, prolonged labour, gestational anemia).
- **Mandatory Action:**
  - Alert receiving triage coordinator.
  - Continuous vital sign reassessment every 15 minutes in transit.

### 3. Low Risk (`#22C55E` / Green)
- **Criteria:** Total MEOWS Score 0 to 1.
- **Clinical Rationale:** Baseline maternal stability maintained.
- **Mandatory Action:** Standard emergency transfer protocol.
